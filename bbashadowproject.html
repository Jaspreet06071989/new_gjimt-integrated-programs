BBA Semester 1: AI-Integrated Shadow Project

GJIMT | Academic Year 2026-27

Introduction

This implementation plan guides BBA students through AI-integrated shadow projects aligned with business fundamentals.

Program Philosophy

  • Clarify business concepts
  • Support data-driven reasoning
  • Enhance communication
  • Develop business competencies

Core Subjects

  • Principles of Management
  • Business Communication
  • Microeconomics
  • Business Mathematics
  • Financial Accounting

AI Integration Framework

AI Tools
  • ChatGPT / Gemini – Concept explanation
  • Copilot / Google AI – Reports
  • Grammarly AI – Writing
  • Excel AI – Data analysis
  • Canva AI – Presentation
AI Usage Principles
  • AI as assistant
  • Verify information
  • Transparency
  • Critical thinking
  • Ethical usage

Project 1: Local Market Study

Benchmark: Reliance Retail

Objective

Analyze demand, customer segments, and pricing.

Deliverables
  • Market Report (1000-1200 words)
  • Excel Data Sheet
  • Presentation (8-10 slides)
  • AI Documentation
Steps
  • Select Business
  • Define Market
  • Conduct Research
  • Segment Customers
  • Analyze Pricing
  • Insights
  • Create Report
Assessment
  • Market Research – 20%
  • Customer Analysis – 20%
  • Pricing – 20%
  • AI Use – 15%
  • Report – 15%
  • Presentation – 10%

Project 2: Customer Communication Audit

Benchmark: Amazon

Objective

Audit business communication quality.

Deliverables
  • Audit Report
  • Sample Analysis
  • Improvement Guide
  • Presentation
Steps
  • Select Business
  • Analyze Communication
  • Find Issues
  • Recommend Improvements
  • Create Guidelines
  • Final Report
Assessment
  • Analysis – 25%
  • Issues – 20%
  • Recommendations – 20%
  • Grammarly AI – 15%
  • Report – 12%
  • Presentation – 8%

General Guidelines

  • Start early
  • Verify data
  • Use professional language
  • Document AI usage
  • Follow ethics

Ethical AI Usage

  • No copying
  • Maintain originality
  • Respect privacy
  • Verify AI outputs

Assessment

  • Total Weightage: 40%
  • Project 1: 20%
  • Project 2: 20%

Grading

  • A+: 90-100
  • A: 80-89
  • B+: 70-79
  • B: 60-69
  • C: 50-59
  • D: 40-49
  • F: Below 40
